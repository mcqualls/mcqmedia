Beards of Iron

The bitter warlords have walled off the lands. Your dwarves have axes, banners, and a grudge.

The game

Siege the walls. Fight the warlord.

Break the fortress

Send color-matched platoons of dwarves at the wall and smash it block by block. Later lands bring their own surprises, like ice, mud that grows back, fog, and powder kegs.

Beat the warlord

At the end of each land, a warlord waits. Pick your heroes, read the boss's tricks, and win a turn-based battle that rewards a good plan.

Gather a legend

Recruit more than 60 heroes, from stubborn dwarves to frog knights and pirates, and gear them up for the fights ahead.

A long campaign

Book One spans 12 lands, 108 fortresses and 12 warlords, with new allies who join you as you free their homes.

Fair to play

Free to play with no energy timers and no forced ads. Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a bonus.

Gifts from the King

King Edvard rewards every day you play. Miss a day and your streak waits for you.

Good to know

Purchases and odds

Beards of Iron is free to download. It offers optional in-app purchases. Hero draws and gear chests are random, and the game lists the odds for every draw in its Shop before you spend anything.
