Support

How can we help?

Email us and a real person will reply, usually within two business days.

support@mcqmediallc.com

To help us fix a problem, tell us which game, your device (for example, iPhone 15), your iOS version, and what happened just before the problem.

Beards of Iron questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. If you delete the game, your progress is deleted with it. Back up your phone to keep it safe.

I bought something but did not get it.

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. If that does not fix it, email us with the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where can I see the odds for hero draws and gear chests?

In the game's Shop. Every random draw lists its odds there before you buy or open it.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads only play when you choose to watch one for a bonus.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the game from your device. We do not keep an account or a copy of your progress. See our Privacy Policy for details.
