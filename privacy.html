Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy explains what information MCQ Media LLC ("we") collects through our games and this website, and how it is used. Our current game is Beards of Iron.

The short version

  • You do not need an account to play.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device. We do not receive it.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • If you choose to watch an optional ad, our ad partner may collect device information, as described below.

Information we collect

Game progress. Your heroes, coins, and progress are stored on your device only. We do not collect or store them on our servers.

Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your name, card number, or billing address. Apple tells the game which item was bought so it can be delivered.

Optional ads. Beards of Iron shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with the ad, to show the ad and measure it. On iPhone, the game asks your permission before allowing tracking. If you say no, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. Learn more in Google's advertising policy.

Emails you send us. If you contact support, we keep your email address and message so we can help you. We do not use them for marketing.

This website. This site has no cookies, analytics, or tracking. Our web host, GitHub Pages, may log technical information such as IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

How we use information

Only to run the game, deliver purchases, show optional ads, answer your messages, and keep our services secure. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Children

Beards of Iron is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Decline tracking when the game asks, or turn it off in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Skip ads entirely. They are always optional.
  • Delete your game data by deleting the game.
  • Ask us to delete any emails you sent us by writing to support@mcqmediallc.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, for example when we add cloud saves, we will update this page and its effective date.
